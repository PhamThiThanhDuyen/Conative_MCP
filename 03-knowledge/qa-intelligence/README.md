# QA Intelligence

Store reusable QA-owned knowledge learned from testing and release work.

## Contents

- `standards/`: required QA methods and quality gates.
- `heuristics/`: exploratory and risk-analysis lenses.
- `defect-patterns/`: recurring failure classes and diagnostic signals.
- `known-issues/`: approved limitations, accepted defects, flaky areas, and environment constraints.
- `evidence-samples/`: sanitized examples of strong evidence artifacts.

QA intelligence improves coverage and execution consistency. It must not invent
or override BA-approved business behavior.
