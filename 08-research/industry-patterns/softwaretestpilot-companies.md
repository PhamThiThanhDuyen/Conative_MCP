# SoftwareTestPilot Companies - Research Recap

- Source: https://softwaretestpilot.com/companies
- Reviewed: 2026-07-30
- Role in the kit: industry pattern radar, not an implementation standard

## Research output

The company guides expose recurring Quality Engineering problem patterns across
large products: GraphQL storefronts, multi-tenant systems, distributed services,
event streaming, payments and fraud, chaos testing, synchronization, and real-time
media. Use these patterns to challenge Conative's capability map and identify
future research gaps; do not copy interview questions into Skills or Knowledge.

## Decision

Keep this source in `08-research` only. Promote a pattern into Knowledge or a Skill
only after it is relevant to a Conative use case and is verified against an
authoritative technical source. The page is interview-preparation content based
partly on candidate reports, so it is not evidence of a company's official QA
architecture or practice.
